Vision-and-Language Navigation (VLN), as one of the core tasks of Embodied AI, is undergoing a paradigm shift from traditional simulation environments to the real physical world. With the explosion of multimodal large models (VLM/VLA), navigation algorithms based on large models are iterating rapidly, and an endless stream of arXiv papers and top-conference works has caused severe "information overload" for researchers.

In actual research and engineering exploration, we have found a significant pain point: **many works achieve SOTA on benchmarks, but often lack consideration for real physical world deployment (Sim2Real).** For developers trying to reproduce algorithms, compare model architectures, or seek deployment guidance, core engineering details (such as real-robot platforms, foundation models, and computational overhead) are often scattered in paper appendices, leading to extremely high information retrieval costs.

To this end, I built and maintain this **comprehensive VLN algorithm overview repository**. This project aims to continuously track and record the latest progress and classic works in this field in a structured manner.
